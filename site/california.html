How this map is made

Each dot is one person counted in the 2020 Census. The Census reports how many people of each group live in every census block (often a single city block). We place each person's dot at a random point inside their block, so dots show where people live at the block level, not at real addresses.

Groups follow the Census question on Hispanic or Latino origin and race. "Hispanic or Latino" includes people of any race; every other group is non-Hispanic. "Native American, Pacific Islander, or other" combines American Indian and Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian and Other Pacific Islander, and Some Other Race.

Where dots overlap, the color is a blend of the groups in that spot, and denser areas appear more solid.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census Redistricting Data (P.L. 94-171) and TIGER/Line block shapes. Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
